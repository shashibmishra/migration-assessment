Key evaluation dimensions:

- **Architecture & Platform Design:** Clear AKS architecture, networking, CNI, ingress, security boundaries, and multi-cluster/tenant considerations.
- **Infrastructure as Code:** Reusable Terraform/Bicep modules, state management, promotion workflows, and drift detection.
- **CI/CD & GitOps:** Reusable pipeline templates, image/build scanning, GitOps repo layout, ArgoCD/Flux configuration, promotion workflows, and rollback paths.
- **DevSecOps:** Image, IaC, and manifest scanning; policy-as-code (Kyverno/OPA); admission controls and pipeline gating.
- **Migration Automation:** Assessment automation, Helm/manifest conversion, migration factory templates, and bulk onboarding processes.
- **Observability & SRE:** SLIs/SLOs, dashboards, alerting, runbooks, and incident response processes.
- **Operational Readiness:** Secrets management, workload identity, RBAC, platform guardrails, and lifecycle management.
- **Presentation & Evidence:** Reproducible demos, GitOps sync screenshots, pipeline logs, security reports, dashboards, and runbooks.
